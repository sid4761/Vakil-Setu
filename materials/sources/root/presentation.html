Problem Statement & Context

The First-Mile Crisis in Access to Legal Justice

Over 50 Million cases are pending across Indian courts. Yet for the average citizen facing a legal dispute, the primary bottleneck is not inside the courtroom—it is knowing where, when, and how to seek help.

01

Domain & Urgency Ambiguity

The First-Mile Confusion

Citizens cannot identify whether their conflict falls under Criminal, Civil, Family, or Property law. Inability to gauge statutory urgency (bail limitation, notice deadlines) leads to irreversible procedural loss.

Impact: Missed limitation periods & forfeited statutory remedies.

02

Linguistic & Jargon Exclusion

The Language Divide

90%+ of legal websites are English-heavy and saturated with legal jargon (IPC/BNS, CrPC, CPC). Semi-urban and rural litigants comfortable speaking in Hindi or regional dialects are entirely excluded.

Impact: Extreme cognitive distress & reliance on predatory touts.

03

Trust Deficit & Opaque Discovery

Unverified Referral Trap

Finding an advocate who genuinely specializes in the dispute domain is a lottery. Litigants face arbitrary consultation fee demands and opaque practitioner credentials with zero performance guarantees.

Impact: Exorbitant upfront fees & mismatched representation.

04

Lack of Accessibility & Triage Tools

Typing vs. Speaking Barrier

Traditional portals require extensive typing and structured legal forms. Under acute stress (threat of arrest, domestic distress, illegal eviction), users struggle to type out structured text.

Impact: Complete paralysis during early stages when settlement is easiest.

Proposed Solution & Value Proposition

Vakil Setu: An Accessible, Voice-First Legal Bridge

An end-to-end, closed-loop legal triage ecosystem that converts unstructured spoken complaints in Hindi or English into structured legal intelligence, verified advocate discovery, and guaranteed consultation booking.

1. Voice Intake & AI Triage

Hands-Free Spoken Input

Native browser Web Speech API allows users to speak in Hindi or English without typing, streaming interim transcriptions in real time.

Statutory Legal Domain Triage

Identifies primary domain (Criminal, Civil, Family, Property) under Indian Law using structured JSON prompt contracts.

Urgency Level Assessment

Scores urgency into Low, Medium, High, or Emergency to highlight critical deadlines and prevent statutory lapses.

Devanagari / English TTS Audio

Speech synthesis speaks action items aloud, ensuring semi-literate citizens understand their immediate legal rights.

2. Advocate Matching & Directory

Domain-Specific Matching

Algorithmic matching pairs litigants only with advocates possessing verified expertise in the identified legal domain.

Transparent Credentialing

Displays verified practice experience (years), court jurisdictions, spoken languages, and real user ratings.

Autonomous Directory Scraping

Cheerio-powered scraper crawls public legal portals (LawRato) to aggregate and update practicing advocate profiles.

Emergency Crisis Escalation

Urgent cases trigger immediate priority routing to the highest-rated active advocate on duty.

3. Payments & Legal Assistant

Direct Consultation Scheduling

Frictionless appointment booking flow linking the user's case brief with the chosen advocate's schedule.

Cryptographic Razorpay Escrow

Server-side HMAC SHA-256 signature verification guarantees secure, transparent consultation fee transactions.

Multi-Turn Case Assistant

Context-aware chat retains the last 10 conversation turns for follow-up inquiries about evidence, fees, and procedures.

Frictionless Guest Sessions

Local storage caching allows immediate legal triage without tedious registration or login barriers.

Technical Approach & Deep Dive

Core Engineering Modules & Implementation Logic

How each module is engineered to provide maximum resilience, privacy, and responsiveness under real-world constraints.

Voice Processing Pipeline

In-Browser Low Latency Speech

  • Zero Server Audio Overhead: Leverages client device processing via webkitSpeechRecognition, eliminating expensive audio cloud upload bandwidth.
  • Dynamic BCP-47 Locale Tagging: Injects hi-IN or en-IN codes based on character-set detection (Devanagari vs Latin).
  • Real-Time Interim Transcription: Streams spoken words live into a draft preview bubble, allowing instant visual feedback.
  • Hands-Free Speech Synthesis: Executes window.speechSynthesis with regional accents for complete auditory loopback.

Dual-Engine Legal Triage

Resilient AI Orchestration

  • Strict JSON Schema Enforcement: Constrains LLM outputs strictly to { domain, urgency, summary, actions } to eliminate parsing errors.
  • Indian Jurisprudence Tuning: Prompt incorporates Indian statutes (BNS/IPC criminal limits, CPC property suits, family laws).
  • Sliding History Window: Formats previous 10 dialogue turns to handle complex multi-turn legal inquiries.
  • Deterministic Rule Fallback: If upstream LLM suffers rate-limiting (429) or gateway timeouts (503), regex heuristics return vetted guidance.

Web Scraping & Data Pipeline

Automated Directory Aggregation

  • Cheerio DOM & LD+JSON Parser: Scrapes directory listings, parsing both embedded schema.org JSON-LD nodes and card DOM structures.
  • Profile-Level Enrichment: Deep-crawls individual advocate profile pages to extract granular experience years, ratings, and domains.
  • Domain Normalization: Maps hundreds of arbitrary legal practice tags into 4 canonical domains (Criminal, Civil, Family, Property).
  • Atomic Upsert Operations: Deduplicates records by normalized advocate names and profile slugs before saving to MongoDB.

Transaction Security & Booking

Cryptographic Escrow Lifecycle

  • HMAC SHA-256 Signature Verification: Verifies Razorpay payment signatures server-side using crypto.createHmac to prevent transaction spoofing.
  • Consultation State Transitions: Implements booking lifecycle states (pending → confirmed / failed) tied to order receipts.
  • Client-Side Session Storage: Sensitive case facts are stored in client LocalStorage, preventing unauthorized database leaks.
  • Emergency Fast-Path: Bypasses scheduling queues to return the highest-rated active advocate on call for urgent crises.

Feasibility & Viability Analysis

Multidimensional Feasibility & Viability Matrix

Evaluating technical deployability, economic sustainability, legal ethics compliance, and operational scalability under Indian conditions.

Technical Feasibility

Client-Side Speech API Efficiency

Eliminates heavy cloud STT server costs. Supported across 80%+ modern browsers (Chrome, Edge, Safari).

Asynchronous Node.js Architecture

Non-blocking I/O easily handles thousands of concurrent triage queries with negligible memory overhead.

Zero-Downtime Rule Fallback

Ensures the platform never fails during emergency situations, even under total external AI API downtime.

Economic Viability

Minimal Operational Overhead

Runs on lightweight serverless/cloud instances; token expenditure is capped by compact prompt design.

Clear Monetization Model

Platform sustains via nominal convenience fee per confirmed consultation booking (5–10%) and advocate directory tiers.

Massive TAM (Total Addressable Market)

Addresses a market of 1.4B citizens with near-zero physical infrastructure requirements.

Legal & Ethical Feasibility

Advocates Act 1961 Compliance

Acts strictly as an informational triage bridge, not a legal practitioner, with prominent non-legal advice disclaimers.

BCI Advertising Rule 36 Adherence

Lawyer listings are objective, factual directories without promotional superlatives or solicitations.

Data Privacy & Client Confidentiality

Litigation secrets are not stored unencrypted; users retain control over case history via session resets.

Operational & Market Viability

High Tier-2 & Rural Penetration

Overcomes the traditional English-only digital barrier, unlocking access for vernacular populations.

Automated Supply Onboarding

Scraping pipeline continuously indexes practicing lawyers without expensive manual data acquisition.

Judicial Decongestion Alignment

Directly complements Govt. of India's Tele-Law and NALSA access-to-justice missions.
